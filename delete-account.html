Request Account Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your OnShift account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

If you have an OnShift account and would like it permanently deleted, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to onshift.app.support@gmail.com, preferably from the email address registered to your OnShift account.
  2. Use the subject line OnShift Account Deletion Request.
  3. State that you would like your OnShift account and associated personal cloud data deleted.
  4. We may contact you to verify account ownership before proceeding.
Request Account Deletion
Processing timeframe

Once account ownership has been verified, we will process your deletion request within 30 days and send confirmation when completed.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Deletion of your OnShift account includes:

  • Your Supabase authentication account and associated account information.
  • Cloud calendars owned by your account.
  • Shift types, roster patterns, date assignments, time overrides, notes and related settings belonging to those calendars.
  • Sharing codes and permissions associated with calendars you own.
  • Your follower and collaboration relationships, subject to the removal of your account.
  • Other account-associated personal cloud records that are not required to be retained.

What Happens to Shared Calendars?

If you own a calendar that other OnShift users follow or edit, deleting your account will also permanently delete that calendar.

Followers and Editors will lose access to those calendars and their associated information.

Calendars owned by other users will not be deleted when your account is removed.

What Data May Be Retained?

Certain limited records may be retained where required by law, for transaction or accounting obligations, security purposes or dispute resolution. Information in service-provider backups may remain until those backups expire under the provider's retention schedule.

Google Play purchase and payment records are managed separately by Google under its own policies. Deleting your OnShift account does not automatically erase records held by Google.

What About Data Stored on My Device?

Account deletion removes the applicable account-associated cloud data. It does not necessarily remove information stored locally on every device where OnShift has been used.

You can remove locally stored application data using your device's app storage settings or by uninstalling OnShift.

Can I Cancel My Deletion Request?

If your request has not yet been completed, contact us as soon as possible to ask whether it can be cancelled. Once the relevant data has been permanently deleted, it may not be recoverable.
