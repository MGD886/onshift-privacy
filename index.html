Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OnShift ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the OnShift mobile application.

OnShift helps users create, manage and share shift-work calendars. Some features work locally without an account, while account-based features use cloud storage and synchronisation.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use OnShift, we process:

  • Account information: Your email address, account identifier and authentication information when you register or sign in.
  • Calendar information: Calendars, shift types, roster patterns, shift assignments, date overrides, times, notes and related settings.
  • Sharing information: Calendar sharing codes, ownership, collaboration permissions and follower relationships.
  • Alarm information: Alarm configurations associated with shifts and dates, and information needed to schedule alarms on your device.
  • Purchase information: Information necessary to verify and maintain your OnShift Pro entitlement.
  • Technical information: Limited technical information that may be processed by our service providers to operate, secure and maintain authentication, cloud storage and purchases.

2. Local and Cloud Storage

You can use OnShift's local calendar features without creating an account. Locally stored calendar information remains on your device unless you choose to use features that transfer it to an account or cloud service.

When you create and use an OnShift account, your account-associated calendars and related information are stored using Supabase cloud services to support synchronisation across authorised devices and calendar sharing.

Cloud calendar changes require connectivity to synchronise reliably. Changes made while offline may not be saved to the cloud.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Save, display and synchronise shift calendars.
  • Enable calendar following and collaboration.
  • Configure and schedule shift alarms.
  • Verify OnShift Pro purchases and restore access.
  • Provide support, maintain security and resolve issues.
  • Process account and data-deletion requests.

4. Calendar Sharing

If you share a calendar, authorised Followers and Editors may access the calendar information made available to their role, including relevant shift schedules and shared notes.

Editors may be permitted to modify shared calendar information. Private information that is not included in the shared calendar is not intentionally disclosed through calendar sharing.

You should avoid including sensitive personal information in shared calendar names, notes or other shared fields.

5. Alarms and Device Permissions

OnShift may request Android permissions or settings access needed to display notifications, schedule alarms and present alarms when the device is locked or another application is open.

These permissions are used to provide the alarm functionality you choose to enable. OnShift does not use alarm permissions for advertising.

6. Purchases

OnShift Pro is offered as a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment transaction. OnShift does not receive or store your full payment card details.

Purchase-related identifiers and entitlement information may be processed to verify your purchase and provide continued access to Pro features.

7. Service Providers

OnShift uses Supabase for account authentication, cloud database services and related infrastructure. Google Play provides app distribution and in-app purchase processing.

These providers may process information according to their own applicable privacy policies and service terms. Information may be processed or stored outside New Zealand, depending on provider infrastructure.

For further information, see Supabase's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Analytics and Advertising

OnShift does not currently include dedicated third-party analytics, advertising tracking or crash-reporting services. We do not sell users' personal information to advertisers.

9. Data Security

We use authentication, access controls and service provider security measures to protect account and calendar information. Password authentication is handled by Supabase Auth; OnShift does not store readable account passwords in its calendar database.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Data Retention

Account and cloud calendar information is generally retained while your account remains active or until it is deleted under our deletion process.

Certain records may be retained for a longer period where necessary to meet legal obligations, address disputes, prevent misuse or maintain required transaction records. Service-provider backups may also have separate retention periods.

Local data may remain on your device until removed through the app, its storage settings or uninstallation.

11. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your OnShift account by contacting onshift.app.support@gmail.com from your registered email address.

We may request additional verification to protect your account. Verified deletion requests will normally be completed within 30 days of verification.

Deleting your account includes deletion of your authentication account and associated personal cloud data, including calendars you own and their associated shifts, patterns, notes and sharing permissions. Followers and Editors will lose access to calendars deleted with your account.

Calendars owned by other users will remain intact. Some information may be retained where legally required or subject to service-provider backup retention.

For full instructions, visit our Account Deletion page.

12. Your Choices and Rights

You can choose to use available local features without registering an account. You can also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

To make a privacy-related request, email onshift.app.support@gmail.com .

13. Children's Privacy

OnShift is designed for shift workers and general roster management, not specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact us.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to OnShift, our service providers or applicable requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

15. Contact

For privacy questions, support or data requests, contact: onshift.app.support@gmail.com .
